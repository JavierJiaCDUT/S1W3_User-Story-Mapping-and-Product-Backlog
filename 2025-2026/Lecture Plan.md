## ⏰ 第二部分：用户故事地图（User Story Mapping）构建 (15 分钟)
## Part 2: Building User Story Mapping (15 minutes)

### 定义与核心思想 | Definition and Core Idea

**中文版：**
- "用户故事地图是一种可视化工具，通过将用户故事按照用户旅程（User Journey）进行排序和组织，来展现产品的全貌。"
- **核心思想：** "骨架"（用户活动/主线任务）→ "行走"（用户步骤）→ "故事"（用户故事）→ "切片"（发布版本）

**English Version:**
- "A user story map is a visualization tool that presents the complete picture of a product by sorting and organizing user stories according to the user journey."
- **Core Idea:** "Backbone" (User Activities/Main Tasks) → "Walking" (User Steps) → "Stories" (User Stories) → "Slices" (Releases)

---

### 构建步骤（结合案例）| Building Steps (with Examples)

#### **步骤一：识别用户活动 (Activities) / 骨架 (Backbone)**
#### **Step One: Identify User Activities / Backbone**

**中文版：**
1. "用户在使用产品时，会经历哪些大的阶段或主要活动？"
   - 例如：登录、浏览商品、发布商品、交易、评价等
2. 用横向排列展示

**English Version:**
1. "What major stages or main activities do users experience when using the product?"
   - For example: Login, Browse products, Post products, Transactions, Reviews, etc.
2. Display horizontally

---

#### **步骤二：细化用户步骤 (Steps) / 行走 (Walking Skeletons)**
#### **Step Two: Refine User Steps / Walking Skeletons**

**中文版：**
1. 在每个活动下，用户会执行哪些具体步骤？
   - 例如：浏览商品下的"搜索商品"、"查看商品详情"、"加入购物车"等
2. 在活动下方纵向排列

**English Version:**
1. Under each activity, what specific steps does the user perform?
   - For example, under "Browse products": "Search products," "View product details," "Add to cart," etc.
2. Display vertically below the activity

---

#### **步骤三：填充用户故事 (User Stories)**
#### **Step Three: Populate User Stories**

**中文版：**
1. 针对每个用户步骤，写出具体的、独立的、有价值的用户故事
2. 在步骤下方继续纵向排列

**English Version:**
1. For each user step, write specific, independent, and valuable user stories
2. Continue to display vertically below the steps

---

#### **步骤四：识别发布版本 (Releases) / 切片**
#### **Step Four: Identify Releases / Slices**

**中文版：**
1. 在地图上划线，将故事切分成不同的发布版本（MVP、V1.0、V2.0等）
2. **强调：** MVP（最小可行产品）是故事地图的第一个切片，包含最核心、能验证价值的故事

**English Version:**
1. Draw lines on the map to slice stories into different releases (MVP, V1.0, V2.0, etc.)
2. **Emphasize:** MVP (Minimum Viable Product) is the first slice of the story map, containing the most core stories that can validate value

---

**中文版：**

## ⏰ 第三部分：将需求转化为可执行的功能列表（Feature）与产品待办事项列表（Product Backlog）(15 分钟)
## Part 3: Transforming Requirements into Executable Feature Lists and Product Backlog (15 minutes)

### 从用户故事到功能 (Feature) | From User Stories to Features

**中文版：**
- "用户故事是用户视角的，功能是开发团队视角的。"
- "一个功能可能由多个用户故事组成，或者一个用户故事是某个功能的具体实现。"
- **例如：** 用户故事"作为一个学生，我想要在课程页面看到我的学习进度条"，可能对应一个"学习进度管理"的功能。

**English Version:**
- "User stories are from the user's perspective; features are from the development team's perspective."
- "A feature may consist of multiple user stories, or a user story may be a specific implementation of a feature."
- **For example:** The user story "As a student, I want to see my learning progress bar on the course page" might correspond to a "Learning Progress Management" feature.

---

### 产品待办事项列表 (Product Backlog)

#### **定义 | Definition**

**中文版：**
- "产品待办事项列表是产品所有已知需求的有序列表，是产品开发的唯一来源。"

**English Version:**
- "The product backlog is an ordered list of all known requirements for a product, and it is the sole source of product development."

#### **构成 | Composition**

**中文版：**
- 用户故事、缺陷、技术债、新功能、优化项等

**English Version:**
- User stories, defects, technical debt, new features, optimization items, etc.

#### **特性 (DEEP 原则) | Characteristics (DEEP Principle)**

| **英文** | **中文** |
|---------|---------|
| **D**etailed appropriately | 细节适当 |
| **E**stimated | 估算过 |
| **E**mergent | 持续涌现/完善 |
| **P**rioritized | 排好优先级 |

---

### 优先级排序 | Prioritization

**中文版：**
- **考虑因素：** 价值/收益、成本/工作量、风险、依赖关系
- **方法：**
  - MoSCoW (Must have, Should have, Could have, Won't have)
  - 加权最短作业优先 (WSJF)

**English Version:**
- **Factors to Consider:** Value/benefit, cost/effort, risk, dependencies
- **Methods:**
  - MoSCoW (Must have, Should have, Could have, Won't have)
  - Weighted Shortest Job First (WSJF)

---

### 持续完善 (Backlog Refinement)

**中文版：**
- 产品待办事项列表不是一成不变的，需要产品经理与团队持续地讨论、细化、估算和排序。

**English Version:**
- The product backlog is not static; it requires continuous discussion, refinement, estimation, and prioritization by the product manager and the team.

**工具 | Tools:**
- Jira, Trello, Asana 等项目管理工具

## ⏰ 第四部分：产品愿景（Product Vision）(5 分钟)
## Part 4: Product Vision (5 minutes)

### 定义与重要性 | Definition and Importance

**中文版：**
- "产品愿景是对产品未来状态的长期、宏伟、鼓舞人心的描述。"
- "它回答了'我们为什么要做这个产品？'和'我们希望这个产品最终变成什么样？'这两个核心问题。"
- **作用：** 为团队提供方向感、凝聚力，指导所有决策。

**English Version:**
- "A product vision is a long-term, ambitious, and inspiring description of the future state of a product."
- "It answers the core questions: 'Why are we building this product?' and 'What do we hope this product will ultimately become?'"
- **Role:** Provides direction and cohesion for the team, guiding all decisions.

---

### 产品愿景声明模板 | Product Vision Statement Template

**Geoffrey Moore 的模板 | Geoffrey Moore's Template:**

```markdown
For [目标客户 | target customer]
Who [有某个需求或痛点 | has a certain need or pain point]
The [产品名称 | product name] is a [产品类别 | product category]
That [关键益处/解决的问题 | key benefit/problem solved]
Unlike [主要竞争对手 | main competitor]
Our product [核心差异化优势 | core differentiated advantage]
```

---

### 示例 | Example

**中文版：**
> "For 寻求便捷出行方式的城市居民，Who 厌倦了交通拥堵和停车难，The Uber is a 按需出行服务，That 通过手机应用连接乘客和司机，提供便捷、高效的出行体验。Unlike 传统出租车，Our product 提供更灵活的调度、更透明的价格和更个性化的服务。"

**English Version:**
> "For urban residents seeking convenient transportation, Who are tired of traffic jams and parking difficulties, The Uber is an on-demand ride-hailing service, That connects passengers and drivers via a mobile app, providing a convenient and efficient travel experience. Unlike traditional taxis, Our product offers more flexible dispatching, more transparent pricing, and more personalized service."

---

### 强调 | Emphasis

**中文版：**
- 愿景是稳定的，但实现愿景的路径（产品路线图、待办事项）是动态调整的。

**English Version:**
- The vision is stable, but the path to achieving the vision (product roadmap, backlog) is dynamically adjusted.

## ⏰ 第五部分：关联 PMBOK 知识领域 (5 分钟)
## Part 5: Relating to PMBOK Knowledge Areas (5 minutes)

### 规划绩效域 | Planning Performance Domain

**中文版：**
- "需求细化"和"产品待办事项列表的构建"是规划过程的核心
- PMBOK 强调通过迭代和渐进的方式进行规划，这与敏捷中产品待办事项的持续完善相契合
- 用户故事地图也是一种可视化规划工具

**English Version:**
- "Requirement refinement" and "product backlog construction" are core planning processes
- PMBOK emphasizes planning through iterative and incremental approaches, which aligns with the continuous refinement of the product backlog in agile
- User story maps are also a visual planning tool

---

### 交付绩效域 - 范围定义 | Delivery Performance Domain - Scope Definition

**中文版：**
- 用户故事地图和产品待办事项列表共同定义了产品的"范围"
- 交付绩效域关注如何有效地交付价值，而清晰定义的需求（通过故事地图和 Backlog）是有效交付的前提

**English Version:**
- User story maps and product backlogs jointly define the "scope" of the product
- The Delivery Performance Domain focuses on how to effectively deliver value, and clearly defined requirements (through story maps and backlog) are a prerequisite for effective delivery

---

### 战略工件 | Strategic Artifacts

**中文版：**
- **产品愿景声明 (Product Vision Statement)：** 是指导产品方向、确保与组织战略一致的关键战略工件
- **路线图 (Roadmap)：** 用户故事地图的切片（Releases）可以被看作是产品路线图的初步形态，它展示了产品在不同阶段的交付计划

**English Version:**
- **Product Vision Statement:** A key strategic artifact that guides product direction and ensures alignment with organizational strategy
- **Roadmap:** The slices (Releases) of the user story map can be seen as the preliminary form of a product roadmap, showing the product's delivery plan at different stages
